AI Agents

AI agents that do the routine work.

An AI agent is software that takes a task from start to finish. It reads what comes in, decides what it is and carries out the next step in your own systems, such as your email, CRM or shop. We build AI agents for owner-led and mid-size businesses in Denmark and abroad.

01, What is an AI agent?

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a program that uses a language model, such as Claude or GPT, to understand a task and carry out its steps in your systems, for example looking up a customer, filling in a record or booking a meeting. It works towards a goal and follows rules for when to stop and ask a person.

Think of it as a new colleague for one narrow job: you give it the goal and the tools it may use. You may also hear this called agentic AI. What sets it apart from ordinary automation, and when it is the wrong choice, is covered in our guide to agentic AI.

04, Rules

Rules we set before an agent goes live.

An agent may only do what you have allowed it to do. This has to be in place before it works on real cases:

  • One clear goal and one number that shows whether the agent works.
  • A list of what it may do on its own and what it must never touch.
  • The steps a person approves before anything leaves the building: outgoing email, changes to customer data and anything involving money.
  • When it must stop and ask: when it is unsure and when the input looks unusual.
  • Which systems and fields it can reach, and nothing beyond that.
  • A log of what the agent has done, so a team member can see and correct what happened.
  • A test on real cases before it comes near a customer.
  • A named person who reviews the results in the first weeks and can switch the agent off.
  • Agreed rules for which data it may use and where that data is processed.

Diagnose, build, improve.

Scope and timeline are fixed before we build. Each agent begins with a bottleneck, a clear goal and the KPIs that show whether it works.

    Build
