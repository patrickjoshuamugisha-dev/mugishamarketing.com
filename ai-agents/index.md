AI Agents

# AI agents that do the routine work.

An AI agent is software that takes a task from start to finish. It reads what comes in, decides what it is and carries out the next step in your own systems, such as your email, CRM or shop. We build AI agents for owner-led and mid-size businesses in Denmark and abroad.

01, What is an AI agent?

## What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a program that uses a language model, such as Claude or GPT, to understand a task and carry out its steps in your systems, for example looking up a customer, filling in a record or booking a meeting. It works towards a goal and follows rules for when to stop and ask a person.

Think of it as a new colleague for one narrow job: you give it the goal and the tools it may use. You may also hear this called agentic AI. What sets it apart from ordinary automation, and when it is the wrong choice, is covered in our [guide to agentic AI](https://mugishamarketing.com/guides/what-is-agentic-ai/).

### Automation, chatbot and AI agent side by side

| | What it does | Who decides the steps | Fits when | Main risk

| Rule-based automation | Runs the same steps every time: if A happens, the system does B. | You, in advance. The steps are written down as rules. | The work does not vary and the input is uniform. | It stalls or gives a wrong result when the input departs from the rules.

| Chatbot | Answers questions in a conversation from the material it has been given. | The visitor picks the topic. The answer comes from your material. | Many people ask the same questions and an answer is enough. | It answers wrongly or vaguely if the material is incomplete.

| AI agent | Reads messy input, picks the next step and carries it out in your systems. | The agent chooses within the limits you set. A person approves the steps you name. | The task has several steps and needs some judgement, such as qualifying an enquiry. | It acts wrongly on its own if the limits are too loose, which is why we set rules before it goes live.

04, Rules

## Rules we set before an agent goes live.

An agent may only do what you have allowed it to do. This has to be in place before it works on real cases:

- One clear goal and one number that shows whether the agent works.

- A list of what it may do on its own and what it must never touch.

- The steps a person approves before anything leaves the building: outgoing email, changes to customer data and anything involving money.

- When it must stop and ask: when it is unsure and when the input looks unusual.

- Which systems and fields it can reach, and nothing beyond that.

- A log of what the agent has done, so a team member can see and correct what happened.

- A test on real cases before it comes near a customer.

- A named person who reviews the results in the first weeks and can switch the agent off.

- Agreed rules for which data it may use and where that data is processed.

### What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions in a conversation. An AI agent also acts: it can update a record, send a draft for approval, book a meeting or pass a case to a person. Many agents have no chat window at all and work in the background on email, forms and your CRM. If you want a chatbot on your website, see our page on an [AI chatbot for your website](https://mugishamarketing.com/ai-chatbot/).

### Can an AI agent work with the systems we already use?

Yes, in most cases. We connect agents to your own systems, such as your email, CRM, calendar or shop, with n8n, Zapier or Make. If a system cannot be connected directly, we look at the options in the diagnose step and tell you what is possible before we build.

### Will an AI agent make mistakes?

Sometimes, as people do. That is why we set the rules before launch: which tasks the agent may do alone, which ones a person approves first and when it must stop and ask. We test it on real cases, and after launch we track the agreed number and improve the agent.

### How much does an AI agent cost, and how long does it take?

Projects start from about 7,000 DKK (roughly USD 1,000), excluding VAT. Both depend on the scope. Each project has a fixed scope and timeline, agreed before work starts, so you know what to expect. The first step is to tell us which task you want off your team’s desk.

### Do we need to hire technical staff for an AI agent?

No. You need one person who knows which task the agent takes over and can approve its first results. We handle the rest of the technical side. Your team can change what we build without code.

### Is our data safe with an AI agent?

We agree up front which data the agent may read and where its output is stored. Tools are picked to match your requirements, such as GDPR and EU hosting, and the agent runs on accounts your business owns. It gets access only to the systems and fields its task needs.
