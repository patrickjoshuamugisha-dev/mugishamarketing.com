AI automation

AI automation that takes routine work off your team.

AI automation uses software and AI models to carry out repetitive business work on its own, such as sorting incoming requests, updating records and drafting replies. A person still decides which steps get checked before anything goes out. We build it on the tools you already use.

01, What it is

Workflows that run themselves.

Classic automation follows fixed rules: when this happens, do that. AI adds the part rules cannot handle. It reads an email, a form or a document, works out what it says and picks the next step. Together they cover work that used to need a person at every stage, which is also called AI workflow automation. When a task needs more judgement and several steps, we build AI agents instead. Both are part of our AI agents and automations service.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Copying details between systems, sorting the inbox, chasing missing information, writing the same reply again and building the weekly report by hand.

What you get

A working workflow with one clear goal and a number we track from the day it goes live. You choose which steps a person approves. Your team can change the workflow without code, and it runs on accounts your company owns.

For example

Say supplier invoices arrive by email as PDFs. The workflow reads each one, pulls out the supplier, date and amount, and enters them in your accounting system. When something looks odd, it sends the invoice to a person with a note on what to check. Nobody types the routine ones.

02, Tools and fit

Built on tools you own.

We pick the tool that fits the job, not the one we like best. Workflows run in n8n, Zapier or Make. Claude and GPT read and write the text. Customer pipelines can live in HighLevel, chat conversations in ManyChat and outreach emails in Instantly. Where your own systems already do the job, we connect to them instead of replacing them. Compare n8n, Make and Zapier, or browse all our AI guides.

Who it is for

Owner-led and mid-size businesses with about 10 to 500 staff, in Denmark and abroad. If your team spends hours each week on the same steps, there is probably something to automate.

How it is set up

Tools are set up on accounts your business owns, so you keep full access. We agree data rules, such as GDPR and EU hosting, before we build.

How we work

A fixed scope and a clear goal.

Before any work begins, we agree the scope, the timeline and the number the automation should move.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We follow the process from start to finish with the people who do it. We find the step that costs the most time and set a goal and KPIs for it.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build the workflow, test it on real cases and set your approval steps. We finish with a documented handover and training.

  3. 03

    Improve

    After launch we track the agreed number, collect feedback and improve the workflow. If you would rather run it yourselves, we train your team to do it.
