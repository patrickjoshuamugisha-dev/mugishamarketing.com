01, AI Agents & Automations

Hand the routine work to AI.

We build AI agents and automations that carry a task from start to finish. They read what comes in, work out what it is and take the next step.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Chasing updates, copying details from one system into another and replying to the same requests day after day.

What you get

A working system with one clear goal, measured from the day it goes live. You decide which steps a person checks before anything goes out.

For example

A new enquiry comes in by email, form or chat. The agent reads it, adds the contact to your records and books the follow-up. It also drafts a reply, which someone on your team checks before it is sent.

02, AI Audit

Want AI, but don’t know where to start?

We talk to the people who do the work, follow each process from start to finish and find where AI will do the most for you.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Guessing which tool to buy and which process to start with.

What you get

A ranked roadmap: what to build first, what comes after it and the return to expect at each step.

For example

Say new leads wait days for a reply and invoices are typed in by hand. The roadmap puts lead follow-up first, because that is where the return is largest, and invoicing second.

03, Custom Software

Software built around how you work.

We design and build the web apps, internal tools and dashboards your team works in every day, and the finished software is yours.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Spreadsheets that have outgrown their job and information scattered across tools that don’t talk to each other.

What you get

One place where the work is tracked, so you can see where things stand at a glance. We build it so new features can be added as your needs change.

For example

One board shows client requests, invoices, stock and the team schedule, so nobody has to ask around for an update.
