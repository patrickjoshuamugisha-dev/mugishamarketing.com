# An n8n consultant for workflows your team can take over.

An n8n consultant sets up, builds and maintains workflows in n8n so that systems, emails and documents work together without manual copy and paste. They choose where n8n runs, test the workflows, put a person in charge of approving what matters and hand everything over documented, so the company’s own team can take over.

01, What we build

## n8n workflows that fit your process.

n8n is a tool that ties systems together in workflows. By its own description you build them visually and can add code where the ready-made steps fall short.

Whether you search for an n8n expert, an n8n developer or an n8n agency, these are the kinds of workflow we build in n8n.

[Talk to us about n8n](https://mugishamarketing.com/contact/)

### Leads and inbox

New enquiries from forms and email land in your CRM and go on to the right person. A model can read an email and sort it as a question, an order or a complaint.

### CRM and documents

Details stay the same across CRM, accounting and spreadsheets, with no double entry. Invoices and other PDFs are read, and supplier, date and amount go into the right system.

### AI agents with approval

An agent can prepare a reply or an action, and a person approves it before anything reaches a customer. n8n itself says you can add human approval where it is needed.

### From a real project

A Danish webshop that also sells on Amazon had us build an AI support agent in n8n. It reads each customer email and its photos, picks the compensation from the owner’s own rules and writes the reply. The owner approves, edits or skips before anything is sent. The agent has run since 25 September 2026 and handled 23 customer cases and 35 replies in its first month.

02, The right tool

## n8n is not always the right choice.

We have no reason to push one tool. If Make or Zapier suits better, we say so and build there. The full comparison is in our guide [n8n vs Make vs Zapier](https://mugishamarketing.com/guides/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier/); here is only the rule of thumb. ### n8n often fits when

Data has to sit in a particular place, the workflow needs its own code, or an internal system has no ready-made connector.

### Make or Zapier often fit when

The person who will adjust the workflow is not technical, or you need to connect many different apps quickly. Then we pick that tool instead.

### Already have an n8n set-up?

We review it first: what runs, on which accounts, and what fails. Then we fix, extend or simplify it, as you choose. Mugisha AI is based in Copenhagen and works with businesses in Denmark and abroad.

03, Hosting and data

## n8n Cloud or your own server: what suits whom

n8n can run with n8n itself or on a server you operate. The choice decides who looks after it and where the data sits.

| Question | n8n Cloud | Your own server

| Who runs it | n8n itself, which handles hosting, updates and scaling. | You, on your own infrastructure, on-premises or in a private cloud.

| Where the data sits | On servers in Frankfurt, Germany. | Wherever you choose to host n8n.

| Updates and maintenance | Handled by n8n. | Your responsibility: you monitor and update it yourselves.

| Cost model (our own prices not included) | A subscription based on monthly executions. An execution is one run of the whole workflow, whatever its steps. | The Community Edition is free, but server and running costs come on top. Paid editions need a licence key.

| Who it suits | Teams without someone to run a server, and where data in Frankfurt is acceptable. | Businesses that want data in their own environment and have someone for the server. The free edition lacks single sign-on and workflow sharing, among other things.

Current plans and prices are on n8n’s own pages, listed under Sources below.

### What has to be in place before we build in n8n

- The process is chosen, and someone on your side owns it.

- The steps can be written down, including those that need judgement.

- You know where data may sit and what data an AI model may see.

- The choice between n8n Cloud and your own server is made, or we make it together.

- If you self-host, someone is responsible for the server and updates.

- Access to the connected systems sits on accounts the company owns.

- You know which steps a person must approve before anything goes to a customer.

- You have a number you can follow from day one: time, cases or errors.

Frequently asked questions

## What people ask when they look for an n8n consultant.

Missing an answer? [Write to us](https://mugishamarketing.com/contact/) and we will help.

### Can you take over an n8n set-up we already have?

Yes. We start by reviewing what runs, on which accounts, and what fails or lacks documentation. Then we agree whether to fix it, build on it or rebuild parts. We need access to your n8n and the systems behind it.

### Do we need a server to use n8n?

Not necessarily. n8n can be hosted by n8n itself, so you do not run a server, or it can run on your own. What suits you depends on where data may sit and whether someone on your side can run a server. See the table above.

### Can n8n use Claude or GPT?

Yes. n8n has a node for Anthropic’s Claude models, and its documentation names OpenAI, behind GPT, and Google as providers. We choose the model for the task and have a person approve what goes to customers.

### Is n8n GDPR-safe?

Hosting alone does not make a workflow GDPR-safe. n8n Cloud stores data in Frankfurt; on your own server it sits where you choose. If a workflow sends text to an AI model, though, it leaves n8n and goes to the model provider. So we agree up front what data each workflow uses.

### Who owns the workflows?

You do. We set n8n up on accounts and servers your company owns, so the workflows and the access sit with you, even if we stop working together.

### Can our team change the workflows afterwards?

Yes. n8n is built visually, and code is only needed where the ready-made steps fall short. We hand over a written description and walk your team through it.
