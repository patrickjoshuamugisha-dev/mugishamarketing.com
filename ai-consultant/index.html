AI consultant

An AI consultant that builds what it recommends.

An AI consultant looks at how your business runs, finds where AI can take work off your team and tells you what to build first. We do that, and then we build it. You get a ranked roadmap, a working system and a team that knows how to run it.

01, What it is

AI consulting in plain words.

An AI consultant reviews how your business works, measures where the time goes and judges which steps AI can take over. The result is a ranked list of what is worth building, what is not and what each item can return. It starts with the work, not with a tool.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Guessing which AI tool to buy, trying things that go nowhere and reading advice that does not fit your business.

What you get

A prioritised roadmap: what to build first, what comes after it and the return to expect at each step. We call this an AI audit.

For example

Say your sales team spends the morning copying orders from email into the system, and quotes go out two days late. We follow one order from inbox to invoice and mark every manual step. The roadmap puts order entry first, because it takes the most hours, and quoting second.

05, AI audit

What an AI audit covers.

An AI audit is the first step of our AI consulting services: a review of how the work actually runs, not a list of tools. We follow one case from the first enquiry to the last step and note where tasks repeat, where data is typed in by hand and where work waits. The result is a ranked roadmap with the number the first solution should move, and a note of what is not worth building. What an audit covers point by point, how to prepare and how to judge a report are in our plain guide to AI audits.

AI consultant, in-house hire or doing it yourself: when each fits

This comparison is our own view, and we have an interest in the answer.

OptionFits whenWatch out for
An external AI consultantYou want to know what is worth building, have one or a few clear tasks and no technical person with time for them. The same people give you the plan and the first build.You are buying a defined piece of work, not a permanent colleague. Choose a consultant who also builds, or you end up with a report and no solution.
An in-house hireThe AI work is continuous and big enough for a full position, and you can attract and keep a person with those skills.The role needs tasks from the first day, and one person is a weak point if they leave.
Doing it yourselves with the tools you haveThe task is small and clear, it is a single process, and someone on the team has both the time and the appetite to try things out.The time comes out of other work, and attempts can stall between a trial and daily use. Write down how the solution works so it does not depend on one person.

In our view, for a business with one or two clear tasks an external consultant is a smaller step than a hire. If you have enough work for a full position all year, an in-house hire is the right call. If we think you do not need us, we will say so.

From audit to running system

Diagnose, build, improve.

The audit has a fixed scope and timeline agreed up front, and the roadmap points at the number the first build should move.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We sit down with the people who do the work and follow one case from the first request to the last step. You get a ranked roadmap, with a clear goal and KPIs for the first build.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build the first item on the roadmap on accounts your business owns. Before it goes live we walk your team through it and hand over written documentation, so they can change it without code.

  3. 03

    Improve

    Once the first item is running we measure it against the agreed number and adjust where the number does not move. If you want to take over, we teach your team to run it.
