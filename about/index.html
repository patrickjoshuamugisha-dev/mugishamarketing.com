Our story
Our story

From putting out fires to preventing them.

Patrick Mugisha began in marketing, working with big international personal brands.

As COO of Fitnesspreneur Academy in the U.S., he ran daily operations and saw how much of a team’s week goes to chasing updates and keeping work on track.

AI showed him a leaner way to scale a business, and he started building it for others.

Today Patrick leads Mugisha AI, and we work directly with business owners on the systems that take that manual work off their teams.
