Our story

## From putting out fires to preventing them.

Patrick Mugisha began in marketing, working with big international personal brands.

As COO of Fitnesspreneur Academy in the U.S., he ran daily operations and saw how much of a team’s week goes to chasing updates and keeping work on track.

AI showed him a leaner way to scale a business, and he started building it for others.

Today Patrick leads Mugisha AI, and we work directly with business owners on the systems that take that manual work off their teams.

Our approach

## What we hold to.

### Know the work first

We learn how the work gets done in your business before we pick a single tool.

### Use AI where it helps

We use AI where it clearly helps, and leave the rest to people.

### Agree the number

Before we build, we agree what the work should return, and we track it after launch.

### Built to be understood

Your team should always know what we built and why it works the way it does.

Working with us

## What to expect from a project.

### A fixed scope

Each project is priced as a fixed scope with a set timeline, agreed before any work begins.

### Your tools and accounts

Everything we build fits into the tools you already use, and we set it up on accounts your business owns.

### A proper handover

Your team can change what we build without touching code, and we finish with documentation and training.

### Care after launch

We stay on to monitor performance, collect feedback and make improvements.
