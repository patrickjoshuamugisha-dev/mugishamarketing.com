# AI guides for businesses

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Plain guides for owners and managers of companies with 10 to 500 staff who want to understand AI before they buy it. Each guide names the pages it is based on, and says so when we could not confirm something.

## Guides

### [What is agentic AI?](https://mugishamarketing.com/guides/what-is-agentic-ai/)

What agentic AI means, how it differs from a chatbot, fixed automation and a single AI agent, with examples, safety steps, EU rules and when to leave it alone.

### [AI in Danish businesses: figures and sources 2026](https://mugishamarketing.com/guides/ai-in-danish-businesses/)

Figures on how many Danish companies use AI, what for and what holds them back, with year, definition and a link to the official source for every number.

### [n8n vs Make vs Zapier: which one fits your business?](https://mugishamarketing.com/guides/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier/)

A side-by-side comparison of hosting, pricing model, AI features and data location for the three most used automation tools, and which kind of business each suits.

### [What is an AI audit?](https://mugishamarketing.com/guides/what-is-an-ai-audit/)

What an AI audit looks at, what you get at the end, how it differs from an AI strategy, workshop or readiness assessment, and how to judge the report.

RPA, workflow automation, AI automation and AI agents compared by how they work, what input they handle and what breaks them, with four worked examples.
