AI implementation

# AI implementation your team will use.

AI implementation means putting AI to work inside your daily operations. We connect it to your email, records and other systems, set the rules for what it may do, and train the people who use it. You end up with a working system and one agreed number that shows whether it pays off.

01, What it is

## AI that works inside your daily work.

AI implementation is the work of taking an AI solution from demo to daily operation: AI integration with your systems, setting rules for what it may do on its own and training the people who use it. It counts as implemented once the work runs through it every day.

[Talk to us about this](https://mugishamarketing.com/contact/)

### The work it removes

Sorting incoming requests, copying details between systems, writing first drafts of routine replies and preparing reports by hand. These tasks follow the same steps every day, so a system can take them.

### What you get

A working system connected to the tools you already use, with one agreed goal and a number we track from launch. You decide which steps a person approves first. Your team can change it without code, and it runs on accounts your company owns.

### For example

Say a distributor gets orders and questions by email, and one person types each into the order system. We connect the inbox to that system. The AI reads each message, fills in the order and flags anything unclear for a person to check. We agree the goal first, for instance the time from received order to confirmed order.

### From a real project

A hotel owner on the Danish coast ran two busy Outlook inboxes. We built an AI email agent in n8n and Microsoft 365 that sorted both inboxes and wrote reply drafts for her to approve. In one week it sorted 121 mails and found the 15 that needed a reply. She told us the drafts were not what she wanted, so we paused them and built what she asked for instead: one mail at 08:00 every morning with the conversations still waiting for her answer.

03, The phases

## The phases of an implementation.

The table shows what happens in each phase, who on your side needs to spend time, and what you hold when the phase ends. We agree the duration once the scope is known.

| Phase | What happens | Who on your side | What you have at the end

| Mapping | We follow the work as it runs today and choose the task that goes first. | The person who does the work today, and someone who can make decisions. | A description of the task, a goal and the number that should move.

| Set-up | We create the accounts, connect the systems and set the rules for what the AI may do on its own. | Whoever administers your systems and can grant access. | A solution connected to your systems that does not yet touch real cases.

| Trial run | We run the solution on real cases with your team alongside and fix what goes wrong. | The team members who will use it. | A test result measured against the agreed goal and a list of fixes.

| Live running | The solution goes into use and a person approves the steps you agreed. | The team that uses it every day. | A solution in daily use and a handover that describes how it works.

| Improvement | We follow the number, collect feedback and adjust where it does not move. | Whoever owns the number. | An updated solution and a number that shows whether it pays off.

### Why AI projects stall, and what we do about it

- **No owner.** Nobody on your side is responsible after launch. We agree an owner on your side before we build.

- **No number.** You cannot see whether it works. We agree one number and measure from day one.

- **A first task that is too big.** The project tries to change everything at once. We start with one well-defined task.

- **The solution does not fit the working day.** The team has to open yet another tool. We connect it to the systems you already use.

- **No trust in the output.** People check everything themselves. We set approval steps and test on real cases with the team.

- **Set up on a personal account.** It stops working when that person leaves. We build on accounts your company owns.

- **No follow-up.** The solution quietly goes out of date. We track the number after launch and make fixes, and your team can change it without code.

### What is AI implementation?

AI implementation is the work of getting AI into a company's daily operations. It covers choosing the task, connecting the AI to your systems, setting what it may do on its own and training your team. A tool you tried once is not implementation. It is in use when the work runs through it every day.

### How long does an AI implementation take?

It depends on the scope. We agree a fixed scope and timeline before work starts, so you know the plan from day one. A single, well-defined task takes much less time than a project that touches several systems.

### What does it cost to implement AI?

Projects start from about 7,000 DKK (roughly USD 1,000), excluding VAT. Each project is priced as a fixed scope, agreed before any work begins. The price depends on how many systems are involved and how much the AI has to do on its own. Tell us about the task and we will tell you what it takes.

### Do we have to replace our existing systems?

No. We connect AI to the systems you already use, such as your email, CRM and accounting tool. If a tool cannot be connected, we say so early and suggest an alternative.

### Is our data safe when we use AI?

Before we connect anything we agree which data the system may see, where it is processed and who has access. Tools must meet your requirements, GDPR and EU hosting for example, and the build sits on accounts your business owns.

### Where should we start if we are unsure?

Start with an AI audit. We talk to the people who do the work, follow each process and give you a prioritised roadmap, so you know what to build first.
