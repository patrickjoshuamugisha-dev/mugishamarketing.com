AI implementation

AI implementation your team will use.

AI implementation means putting AI to work inside your daily operations. We connect it to your email, records and other systems, set the rules for what it may do, and train the people who use it. You end up with a working system and one agreed number that shows whether it pays off.

01, What it is

AI that works inside your daily work.

Most AI projects stall between the demo and daily use. Implementation is the part in between: connecting the AI to your systems, setting the rules and training the people who use it.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Sorting incoming requests, copying details between systems, writing first drafts of routine replies and preparing reports by hand. These tasks follow the same steps every day, so a system can take them.

What you get

A working system connected to the tools you already use, with one agreed goal and a number we track from launch. You decide which steps a person approves first. Your team can change it without code, and it runs on accounts your company owns.

For example

Say a distributor gets orders and questions by email, and one person types each into the order system. We connect the inbox to that system. The AI reads each message, fills in the order and flags anything unclear for a person to check. We agree the goal first, for instance the time from received order to confirmed order.

02, Tools and fit

Built with tools you can keep.

We build with n8n, Zapier and Make for the automations, Claude and GPT for the AI, HighLevel, ManyChat and Instantly where leads and outreach are involved, and your own systems around them. We pick what fits your case.

Not sure where to start? An AI audit ranks the options first, and you can read about our AI consultant work. See also how we build AI agents and AI automation, or browse all our services.

Who it is for

Owner-led and mid-size businesses with about 10 to 500 staff, in Denmark and abroad. Typically a team where the same manual tasks eat hours every week and nobody has time to build the fix.

Your accounts, your data

Tools are set up on accounts your company owns. We agree data rules, such as GDPR and EU hosting, before we start, so you know where your data goes.

How the deal works

Every project has a fixed scope and timeline, agreed before work starts. It ends with a documented handover and training, so your team can carry on.

How an implementation runs

A clear goal before we build.

Before any work begins, we agree the scope, the timeline and the number the project should move.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We talk to the people who do the work and follow each process from start to finish. We find the bottleneck, set a clear goal and agree the KPIs before we build anything.

  2. 02

    Build

    We connect the tools, set the rules for what the AI may do alone and what a person checks, and test it with your team. We finish with a documented handover and training.

  3. 03

    Improve

    After launch we track the agreed number, collect feedback and make improvements. If you would rather run it yourselves, we train your team to do it.
