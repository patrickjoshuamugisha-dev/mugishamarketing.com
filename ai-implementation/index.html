AI implementation

AI implementation your team will use.

AI implementation means putting AI to work inside your daily operations. We connect it to your email, records and other systems, set the rules for what it may do, and train the people who use it. You end up with a working system and one agreed number that shows whether it pays off.

01, What it is

AI that works inside your daily work.

AI implementation is the work of taking an AI solution from demo to daily operation: connecting it to your systems, setting rules for what it may do on its own and training the people who use it. It counts as implemented once the work runs through it every day.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

Sorting incoming requests, copying details between systems, writing first drafts of routine replies and preparing reports by hand. These tasks follow the same steps every day, so a system can take them.

What you get

A working system connected to the tools you already use, with one agreed goal and a number we track from launch. You decide which steps a person approves first. Your team can change it without code, and it runs on accounts your company owns.

For example

Say a distributor gets orders and questions by email, and one person types each into the order system. We connect the inbox to that system. The AI reads each message, fills in the order and flags anything unclear for a person to check. We agree the goal first, for instance the time from received order to confirmed order.

02, Tools and fit

Built with tools you can keep.

We build with n8n, Zapier and Make for the automations, Claude and GPT for the AI, HighLevel, ManyChat and Instantly where leads and outreach are involved, and your own systems around them. We pick what fits your case.

Not sure where to start? An AI audit ranks the options first, and you can read about our AI consultant work. See also how we build AI agents and AI automation, or browse all our services.

Who it is for

A team of about 10 to 500 people, in Denmark or abroad, where the same manual tasks eat hours every week and nobody has time to build the fix. For the national figures on how many Danish companies already use AI, see our facts page.

Your accounts, your data

Tools are set up on accounts your company owns. We agree data rules, such as GDPR and EU hosting, before we start, so you know where your data goes.

How the deal works

Scope and timeline are fixed before work starts. The project ends with documentation and training, so your team can carry on.

Why AI projects stall, and what we do about it

  • No owner. Nobody on your side is responsible after launch. We agree an owner on your side before we build.
  • No number. You cannot see whether it works. We agree one number and measure from day one.
  • A first task that is too big. The project tries to change everything at once. We start with one well-defined task.
  • The solution does not fit the working day. The team has to open yet another tool. We connect it to the systems you already use.
  • No trust in the output. People check everything themselves. We set approval steps and test on real cases with the team.
  • Set up on a personal account. It stops working when that person leaves. We build on accounts your company owns.
  • No follow-up. The solution quietly goes out of date. We track the number after launch and make fixes, and your team can change it without code.
How an implementation runs

A clear goal before we build.

Before we build anything, it is fixed what the project covers, when it is done and which number has to move to show it works.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We start with the people who do the work and trace each process end to end. We find the bottleneck, set a clear goal and agree the KPIs before we build anything.

  2. 02

    Build

    We connect the tools, set the rules for what the AI may do alone and what a person checks, and test it with your team. The handover comes with written documentation and training for the people who will use it.

  3. 03

    Improve

    After launch we track the agreed number, collect feedback and make improvements. If your team takes over the running, the training is part of the project.
