Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Mugisha Marketing (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates internationally with its primary entity based in Denmark. We provide AI agents and automations, AI audits, custom software, and web development services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

We may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business information
  • Information submitted through booking forms or contact forms

b. Automatically Collected Data

We do not actively use tracking tools such as pixels or advanced analytics. However, basic technical data (e.g. IP address, browser type) may be collected through hosting systems.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule calls
  • Deliver our services
  • Communicate with you regarding your business
  • Improve our services

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, we process your data based on:

  • Your consent (e.g. when submitting forms)
  • Legitimate interest (business communication and service delivery)
  • Contractual necessity (when working with clients)

4. Data Sharing

We may share data with:

  • AI providers (e.g. OpenAI, Google Gemini)
  • CRM systems (when integrating with your systems)
  • Service providers necessary to operate our business

All third parties are expected to handle data securely.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary:

  • For communication and relationship management
  • For active client engagements
  • To comply with legal obligations

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact: patrick@mugishamarketing.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

8. International Data Transfers

As we operate globally, your data may be processed outside your country, including outside the EU. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place where required.

9. Cookies

We currently do not actively use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
