## What business owners ask before they start.

Something else on your mind? Write to [patrick@mugishamarketing.com](mailto:patrick@mugishamarketing.com).

### What does it cost, and how long does it take?

Projects start from about 7,000 DKK (roughly USD 1,000), excluding VAT. Both depend on the scope. Each project is priced as a fixed scope with a set timeline, agreed before any work begins. You know what to expect.

### What if AI doesn't work for us?

We only build where the return is clear. Every build starts with a bottleneck, a clearly defined goal and KPIs that prove its value.

### Do we need technical people in-house?

No. We handle the technical side from start to finish. Everything we build fits into the tools you already use. Your team can change it without touching code, and we end with a documented handover and training.

### What happens after launch?

We stay on to monitor performance, collect feedback and make improvements. If you prefer to run it yourselves, we teach your team to do the same.

### Is our data safe?

Your data is handled on your terms. We agree up front which data each build uses and where it goes. We choose tools that meet your requirements, such as GDPR and EU hosting, and set everything up on accounts your business owns.
