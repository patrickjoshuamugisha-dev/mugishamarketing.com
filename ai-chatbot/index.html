AI chatbot

An AI chatbot for your website that answers like your team.

An AI chatbot for your website answers visitors’ questions in plain language, using your own prices, policies and product information. It can also take down details, book a meeting and pass the conversation to a person when it should. We build it, connect it to your systems and keep improving it after launch.

01, What it is

A chatbot that knows your business.

Older chatbots follow a fixed menu: pick an option, get a canned reply. An AI chatbot reads the question as the visitor wrote it and finds the answer in the material you give it, such as your FAQ, price list, product pages and policies. If the answer is not there, it says so and offers to pass the question to your team. It is customer service that keeps answering outside opening hours.

Talk to us about this

The work it removes

The same questions arriving by email, phone and chat: opening hours, delivery, what is included and how to book. It also takes the first round of back and forth with new leads, so your team starts the conversation further along. When the job goes beyond answering, the chatbot can be the front of an AI agent that acts on the request.

What you get

A chatbot on your website, built on your own information and set up to match your tone. It collects the details your team needs, books meetings in your calendar and hands over to a person with the whole conversation attached. You decide which topics it handles and which it never touches.

For example

Say a company that installs heat pumps gets the same questions about timing, how long an installation takes and what a visit involves. The chatbot answers from the company’s own material. A visitor who wants a quote leaves an address and a phone number, and the chatbot books a call. The salesperson can read the conversation before dialling.

02, Tools and fit

Built with tools you can see.

We choose the language model and the chat tool that suit your setup, and connect them to the systems your team already uses.

Talk to us about this

Tools we build with

Claude or GPT reads the question and writes the answer. n8n, Zapier or Make connect the chat to your systems. HighLevel handles contacts and bookings, and ManyChat covers chat on social channels. We also work with your own systems, such as your CRM, helpdesk or booking tool.

Who it is for

Owner-led and mid-size businesses, from about 10 to 500 staff, in Denmark and abroad. It fits best where customers ask many of the same questions and a few people spend part of every day answering them. Not sure a chatbot is the right first step? An AI audit, part of our AI consulting, ranks what to build first.

Your accounts, your data

Everything is set up on accounts your business owns. We agree up front which data the chatbot may use and where it is stored, including GDPR and EU hosting. See all our services, or read about AI automation if you want more than a chatbot.

How a project runs

A measurable goal first, then the chatbot.

Before any work begins, we agree the scope, the timeline and the number the chatbot should move, such as questions answered without your team or meetings booked.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We read the questions your team answers today, from email, phone and chat, and pick the ones a chatbot should take. We also agree where it must hand over to a person.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build the chatbot on your information, connect it to your calendar and records, and test it with real questions. We finish with a documented handover and training.

  3. 03

    Improve

    After launch we read the conversations, track the agreed number and fix the gaps. If you would rather run it yourselves, we train your team to do it.
